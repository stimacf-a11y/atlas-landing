# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Street Lab
- **Category**: Contemporary premium streetwear (apparel, denim, eyewear, footwear, objects)
- **Description**: An urban culture laboratory creating contemporary objects for life in the street. Treats street culture as an R&D input, turning it into product via "experiments," "drops," and "objects."
- **Stage**: Pre-launch — full brand book (v1.0) written; visual identity partially produced (script logotype variants exist); no live site, social, or product yet.
- **Website**: N/A

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Core 18–35 (streetwear, sneakers, music, art, design, photography, skate, digital culture, travel). Extended 35–50 (culturally connected, wants sophisticated streetwear without dressing "too young" or abandoning the urban aesthetic).
- **Key Problem**: Wants differentiation and cultural fluency without costume-like trend-chasing; wants premium feel without full luxury pricing.
- **Their Language**: Not directly researched yet (no customer interviews/reviews) — vocabulary is constructed top-down from the brand's own lexicon (object, experiment, drop, unit, series, specimen).

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: "The street is the lab" — urban culture treated literally as ongoing experimentation, expressed through object/experiment/drop numbering, lab-report-style information design, and a controlled-rawness visual language.
- **Competitors**: Not named in the source document. Implied territory: premium streetwear / contemporary fashion (Stüssy referenced directly as the visual/typographic reference for the logotype).
- **Market Position**: Accessible premium — between mass streetwear and full luxury.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Experimental, urban, intelligent, independent, confident, raw, premium, international, contemporary, culturally aware
- **Tone**: Direct, confident, minimal, occasionally provocative — never over-explains
- **Voice Admires**: Not specified beyond Stüssy as a visual/lettering reference

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Experimentation over trend-following, craft/desirability/wearability/distinctiveness per product, "never decorate without purpose," restraint over noise
- **Mission**: Design contemporary urban products with a strong visual identity, quality materials, and a narrative of cultural experimentation. Archetype mix: 65% Creator / 35% Rebel.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Establish a coherent, ownable identity system (Ø symbol, tagline, product/drop naming) before launch; grow from apparel/denim/eyewear (Phase 1) toward a full cultural platform (Phase 5).
- **Key Metrics**: Not yet defined — no `brand-measurement` work done.
